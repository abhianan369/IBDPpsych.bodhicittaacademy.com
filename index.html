Questions

Is this for SL or HL?

Both. Under the 2027 syllabus the research proposal IA is the same task at both levels. It carries 30% of your grade at SL and 20% at HL.

I'm in DP1, sitting exams in 2028. Is it too early?

The opposite. Thinking about your population and problem early is the single biggest advantage the new format offers, because the proposal rewards depth of decision-making rather than last-minute writing.

Is the kit really free?

Yes, and so is the IA Clinic. We run paid online batches, one-to-one IA mentoring and exam crash courses, but you'll never need any of them to use this.

Do you guarantee a 7?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. What we offer is guidance built on the current syllabus and honest feedback on your work.
